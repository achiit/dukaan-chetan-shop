About the shop

I opened this shop in 2016 with a small shelf of school bags. My cousin helped me carry the first stock on his bike, and I still remember how nervous I was on the first day.

Today many of my customers are the same families, now buying for their children's children. That is why I never sell a bag I would not use myself. If a zip or strap gives trouble, bring it back and we will sort it out.

Come to Madhav Nagar, hold the bag in your hands, and take your time. There is no pressure here.

  • Check every zip and stitch before you pay
  • Bags for every budget, from school to travel
  • Owner is always at the shop
